# Manifesto

## What we believe

**1. Government is infrastructure.** Like roads, like power grids, like the internet. When it stops working for ordinary people, you fix it. You don't worship it.

**2. The current system is captured.** Corporations and the very wealthy write the laws through lobbyists, campaign donations, and revolving-door hires. Working people get the bill. This isn't a conspiracy. It's the public record.

**3. Representation has failed as a delivery mechanism.** 535 people in Washington cannot represent 330 million Americans. They were never going to. In 1789 it was the best we had. In 2026 we have better tools.

**4. The Constitution is a starting point, not a sacred text.** The founders wrote in amendments because they knew they didn't have all the answers. We owe them the honesty of using that mechanism.

**5. Plain language is a civil right.** If you can't read your own laws, you don't really live under them — you live under whoever can.

**6. Direct democracy is now technically possible.** We can verify identity, we can secure votes, we can deliberate at scale, and we can use AI to help every citizen draft legislation as competently as a K Street lawyer.

**7. Working-class people deserve a government that works for them.** Housing, healthcare, education, dignity at work, a living wage. Not as charity. As rights.

## What we reject

- **Lobbying as legalized bribery.** It ends.
- **Campaign finance as auction.** Public funding only.
- **Gerrymandering.** Proportional representation, full stop.
- **Lifetime judicial appointments.** Term limits. Binding ethics.
- **Permanent law without review.** Every law sunsets. Renew it or lose it.
- **Opacity.** Every meeting, vote, draft, and dollar is public by default.

## What we're building

A constitutional slate. A process. A repository. A movement that treats the country like the working system it should be — open, forkable, debuggable, ours.

## The bet

That if we write it clearly, structure it openly, and make it easy to participate, Americans will show up. Because most of us already agree on most of this. We've just never had a place to say so together.

This is that place.
