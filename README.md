# we-the-repo

**Government as open source.**

> _People over politics. Truth over power._

This is a draft of a new American government — written in plain English, version-controlled, and open to every citizen as a contributor.

## The problem

The U.S. federal government is captured. Corporations and their lobbyists write the laws. Wealth decides what passes. Working-class people lose. The Constitution itself is written in language most Americans can't read without a translator.

We can do better. We have to.

## The idea

Run government the way modern software gets built:

- **Source control.** Every law has a history. You can see who changed what, when, and why.
- **Pull requests.** Any citizen can propose a change. The change gets reviewed, debated in the open, and merged or rejected on the record.
- **Consensus mechanisms.** Decisions are made by the people affected, not by a few hundred representatives bought by donors.
- **Automation.** AI helps draft, analyze, and explain legislation — so a single citizen has the same drafting power as a corporate legal team.
- **Agile releases.** Government ships in small, reviewable increments. Every law has a sunset. Nothing runs forever without re-review.

## Reading level

Every word in this repo is written so any American with an 8th-grade reading level can understand it. No Latin. No legalese. No nested subclauses. If you need a law degree to read your own rights, those aren't really your rights.

## How to contribute

See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md). Short version: fork it, change it, send a pull request. Argue your case in the open. We'll merge what makes the country better.

## Who's behind this

Citizens. Working people. Engineers, teachers, nurses, drivers, parents. Anyone who's tired of waiting for Washington to fix itself.
