## How to read these

Each amendment has three parts:

1. **The text.** What goes into the Constitution. Plain English.
2. **Plain-language commentary.** What it means in everyday terms.
3. **Rationale.** Why we need it. What problem it solves.

## Ratification

These amendments are drafts. They get ratified the way the Constitution says — proposed by two-thirds of both houses of Congress (or a convention of the states), and ratified by three-fourths of state legislatures (or state conventions).

We are not pretending to amend the Constitution by writing Markdown. We are doing the hard part first: agreeing, in public, on what we actually want.

The political work comes next.
